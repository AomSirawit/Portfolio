# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Potential employers, clients, and fellow developers looking for a professional and capable full-stack engineer. They value clarity, technical depth, and a clean aesthetic.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio website that showcases Sirawit Makdee's skills, work history, and contact information. The goal is to establish professional credibility and provide a seamless way for interested parties to get in touch.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Minimalist, Confident. The voice is direct and expertise-driven, while the visual style is clean and focused.

## Anti-references
Overly saturated "SaaS-cream" palettes, cluttered layouts, generic hero-metric templates, and unnecessary animations that distract from the content.

## Design Principles
1. **Content First**: Every design choice should serve to make the information clear and readable.
2. **Minimalist Sophistication**: Use negative space and typography hierarchy to create a premium feel.
3. **Intentional Contrast**: Use the dark background and accent colors to guide the user's attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. High contrast for readability, keyboard navigation support, and reduced motion alternatives for any animations.
